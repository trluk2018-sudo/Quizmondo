Minimum age to sign up

13 is the default. UK law — the Data Protection Act 2018 — sets 13 as the age a child can consent to an online service, so going lower is a decision to take advice on. Higher is always allowed.

Worth knowing. Existing accounts are untouched by any change here — nobody is asked again and nobody is removed. Turning the gate off only affects new signups from that moment.
