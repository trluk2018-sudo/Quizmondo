## 4. Cold Outreach Deliverability & Sending Rules

When sending bulk cold emails, do **not** send 200 emails directly from your personal Gmail or main MX server on Day 1.

### Step 1: Outreach Domain Setup
* **Do not use your root operational domain** (`quizmondo.com`) for cold blasts.
* Purchase a sending alias domain: e.g., `quizmondomedia.com`, `getquizmondo.com`, or `quizmondopartners.co.uk`.
* Set up **Google Workspace** or **Microsoft 365** accounts on that secondary domain.

### Step 2: DNS Authentication
Ensure the sending domain has:
* **SPF**: `v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all`
* **DKIM**: 2048-bit key generated in Workspace Admin
* **DMARC**: `v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@quizmondomedia.com`

### Step 3: Warm-up Period
* Connect the inbox to **Instantly.ai** or **Smartlead.ai**.
* Turn on automated warm-up for **14 days** before sending any cold volume.
* Ramp-up sending:
  * Week 3: 15 emails/day
  * Week 4: 25 emails/day
  * Max volume: 35–40 cold emails per inbox per day.
  * To send 200/day, use 5 sender accounts across 2 secondary domains.
